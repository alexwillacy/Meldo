The creation loop

Social, but built around making.

Most platforms stop at posting. MELDO is designed for the next step: giving someone else a way into the idea.

01 · PostShare an idea, sketch, riff, scene, concept or work in progress.
02 · DiscoverFind work, people and projects across creative disciplines.
03 · BuildUse “Build on This” to contribute your craft to something unfinished.
04 · CollaborateBring contributors together around one living project.
05 · CreditShow who made what and preserve the project's creative lineage.

FAQ

Before we open.

Is MELDO live?

Not yet. The interactive prototype demonstrates the intended experience, but accounts, uploads and collaboration data are not currently persistent.

Will creators have to pay?

The core vision is a free platform for creators. Any future optional commercial features would be clearly separated from basic participation.

Who owns collaborative work?

That framework is being developed before public uploads open. MELDO will need clear contribution, permission, credit and ownership rules for collaborative projects.

Can I try the product?

Yes. The prototype is interactive and can be explored now, but it should be treated as a demonstration rather than a production service.
