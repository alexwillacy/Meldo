Prototype terms

MELDO is currently a concept and product prototype, not a production social network.

The prototype contains fictional/example profiles, projects and interactions for demonstration purposes. Buttons and workflows may simulate future functionality without creating accounts, contracts, licences, ownership rights or real collaborations.

Before creators can upload or collaborate on real work, MELDO will require production terms covering account rules, user content, licences, collaborative contributions, credits, intellectual property, moderation, takedowns, disputes and termination.

This page is a prototype notice and is not a substitute for final legal terms reviewed for the actual service.
